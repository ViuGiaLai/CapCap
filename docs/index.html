Features

Everything you need to localize videos

From transcription to voiceover, CapCap Studio streamlines the entire workflow.

AI Transcription

SenseVoice, Whisper, Faster‑Whisper with speaker diarization for accurate, timestamped subtitles.

AI Translation

Translate subtitles using OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepL, or local LLMs with style presets.

Voice Synthesis

EdgeTTS, CosyVoice, F5‑TTS, MeloTTS for natural, multi‑speaker voiceovers.

Multi‑Track Timeline

Edit video, audio, subtitles, blur, logo, mask, and text layers on a flexible timeline.

Style & Effects

Custom typography, colors, outlines, animations, and visual overlays.

GPU Accelerated

Leverage CUDA for 5‑10x faster transcription, translation, and voice generation.
